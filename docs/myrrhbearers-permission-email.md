# Draft — permission request to Holy Myrrh-bearers

**To:** e-mail@myrrh-bearers.org
**Subject:** Permission to use Holy Myrrh-bearers texts in our parish service generator

---

Dear Fathers/Brethren at Holy Myrrh-bearers,

Christ is in our midst!

Our parish uses your liturgical materials for our services, and we are building a
small in-house tool that assembles the daily service texts (Vespers, Matins, and
the Divine Liturgy) for our clergy and choir to print each week. We would like the
texts it produces to match what we actually sing — your Sunday Octoechos
translation and the wording of your service booklets.

May we have your permission to include your texts in this tool for our parish's own
liturgical use? To be clear about scope:

- It is for our parish's internal use (clergy, choir, and faithful), not for resale
  or public redistribution.
- We would attribute the texts to Holy Myrrh-bearers wherever they appear.
- We are glad to limit it to whatever you are comfortable with — a specific set of
  services, a specific list of texts, or a time-limited trial — and to honor any
  conditions you place on it.

If it is easier, we are also happy to speak by phone or email about the details.
Thank you for the labor you have put into making these texts available; it is a
real help to parishes like ours.

With gratitude and asking your prayers,

[Your name]
[Parish name and location]
[Contact]

---

*Notes for the sender:*
- Their site posts no reuse license, so explicit permission matters. Framing it as
  parish-internal, attributed, and scope-flexible makes a yes easy.
- If they ask what "the tool" is, it's a private service-text generator for the
  parish; you can offer to show it to them.
- If they grant only part (e.g. Octoechos but not the booklets), that still unblocks
  the highest-value phase.
