Lord I Have Cried
Tone 6 (to the fathers)
The seven hon' rable councils of the Fathers,
held at var-ious times,
were brought together under one canon in good order,
by Patriarch Germanus the New.
He established and recorded their teachings,
presenting these Fathers to the Lord
as watchful intercessors for our salvation//
and as fellow-shepherds of the fold.
The book of the law worthily honored the seventh day for Israel,
veiled in prefiguring shadow,
but Fathers, by your participation in the Seventh Council,
through the inspiration of God
Who completed the universe in six days and blessed the seventh,/ /
you have honored it more greatly by decreeing the bounds of faith!
You have enabled all men, O thrice-bless-èd Fathers,
to come to the knowledge of the Trinity by Its works,
for by your mystical speech
you have appeared as cham-pions of the Orthodox Word,/ /
proving that the Trinity is truly the Creator of the world!
Sunday of the Holy Fathers (October 7-11) 1
Vespers

Lord I Have Cried (continued)
Tone 6 (Same Tone)
It would have been enough for Elijah the Prophet
to have bent only once to give life to the dead son of the woman;
but he knelt and bent seven times.
And in his foresight he prophesied your gathering,
reviving in the Church the Incarnation of the Word of God,/ /
while putting to death the A-ri-an heresy.
In your wisdom, ven' rable Fathers,
you mended the garment of Christ rent by howling dogs;
for you could not bear looking upon the nakedness of His body,
but as Shem and Japheth of old,
you confounded those who would not respect their own father//
foolishly following the teachings of A-ri-us.
You expelled the vicious heretics from the Savior's fold,
those de-vour-ing wolves clothed in the skins of lambs:
Truly, you have done well, faithful shepherds, in exposing the
wick-ed ones://
Therefore we all glorify you!
Sunday of the Holy Fathers (October 7-11) 2
Vespers
