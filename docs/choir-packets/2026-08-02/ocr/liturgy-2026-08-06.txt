moved, but abides forever.
Troparion (Thou wast transfigured...)
Verse: As the mountains are round about Him, so the Lord is round about
His people, from this time forth and forevermore.
Troparion

holy hill?

BLESSING OF GRAPES AND FRUIT
Immediately after the Prayer before the Amvon, according to ancient custom, we
observe the blessing of grapes as well as apples and other fruit. The fruit is censed
as we sing:
• Troparion, Feast, Tone 7: Thou wast transfigured on the Mount, O Christ God, /
revealing Thy glory to Thy disciples as far as they could bear it. / Let Thine
everlasting light shine upon us sinners! // Through the prayers of the Theotokos, O
Giver of Light, glory to Thee!
Glory... now and ever...
• Kontakion, Feast, Tone 7: On the mountain wast Thou transfigured, O Christ God,
/ and Thy disciples beheld Thy glory as far as they could see it; / so that when they
would behold Thee crucified, / they would understand that Thy suffering was
voluntary, / and would proclaim to the world // that Thou art truly the Radiance of
the Father!
Deacon: "Let us pray to the Lord."
Choir: "Lord, have mercy."
Priest: "Bless, O Lord, this new fruit of the vine..." (Prayer at the Partaking of
Grapes; printed in The Festal Menaion, p. 502; also in The Great Book of Needs,
Volume 2, p. 352)
Choir: "Amen."
Deacon: "Let us pray to the Lord."
Choir: "Lord, have mercy."
Priest: "O Master, Lord our God..." (Prayer for Them That Offer First Fruits;
printed in The Great Book of Needs, Volume 2, p. 353)
Choir: "Amen."
The priest sprinkles the grapes and fruit with holy water, saying: "These grapes
and fruit are blessed and sanctified by the sprinkling of this holy water, in the
name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. " (thrice)
Choir: "Blessed be the name of the Lord, henceforth and forevermore" (thrice).
And the rest as usual.
