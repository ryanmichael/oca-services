The weekly sheet carries everything from there through the Gospel, with readings given as citations only. Pages are drawn at true size, 5.5 × 8.5 in, in the booklet's own type.

How it folds

  • Print Letter landscape, two-sided, flip on short edge. Fold once. No staple needed for a single sheet.
  • The sheet lies loose inside the book's front cover. There is no page it has to be filed into.
  • Page numbers 8 and 14 are placeholders. The real ones come from the printed book, and the footer's edition tag lets the app warn if the book and the sheet ever disagree.
  • Signposts are solid black and the rest is the booklet's usual ink and rubric red, so it reads on a black-only laser.
