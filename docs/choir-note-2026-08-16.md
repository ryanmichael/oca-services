# Choir note — Divine Liturgy, Sunday 16 August 2026

11th Sunday after Pentecost · **Tone 2** · Afterfeast of the Dormition ·
Translation of the Image "Not-Made-by-Hands"

Three things the printed sheet leaves out, one thing that changed since last
week, and one question. The rest of the service is complete and matches the OCA
order for the day.

---

## 1. Three gaps — you'll need your own books

### The second prokeimenon and the second alleluia are not printed

The sheet gives the Sunday pair only:

> Prokeimenon, Tone 2 — "The Lord is my strength and my song…"
> Alleluia, Tone 2

The OCA order appoints a **second set for the Image, both Tone 4** — the
prokeimenon beginning **"Sing to the Lord a new song…"**

We don't print them because the OCA order names them by their opening words
only, and I haven't been able to find the full verses in any source we have. I'd
rather leave a visible hole than print something invented. **If you have them in
a book, I'd be glad to have them** — send me the text and the psalm reference and
they'll be on the sheet permanently.

> ⚠️ One warning, in case you go looking in our system: the Image *does* have a
> Tone 4 prokeimenon printed at **Matins** ("O Lord, in the light of Thy face
> shall we walk…"). That is a different text. The matching tone number makes it
> look like the answer and it isn't.

### Two Beatitude troparia are missing

The order appoints twelve troparia; we print ten. Missing are the **two from Ode
1 of the *first* canon of the Dormition, Tone 1**. We simply don't have that
canon's troparia — the book we have carries only its irmoi.

They belong **after the four Resurrection troparia and before the two Dormition
Tone 4 ones.** Because we're at ten rather than twelve, the troparia currently
begin at *"Blessed are the meek."* If you supply the missing pair, start one
verse earlier, at *"Blessed are the poor in spirit."*

---

## 2. What changed this week

**The Beatitudes are blended now.** Until this morning we printed the plain
Octoechos set — six resurrection troparia, then Glory and the Theotokion. The
sheet now follows the OCA order for the day:

| | |
|---|---|
| 4 troparia of the Resurrection | Tone 2 |
| *(2 of the Dormition, 1st canon — missing, see above)* | *Tone 1* |
| 2 of the Dormition, 2nd canon | Tone 4 |
| 4 of the Image — its last is the Theotokion, at "Now and ever…" | Tone 4 |

If the choir has always sung the plain Octoechos set here, say so and I'll put
it back — see the question at the end.

Also: the Tone 2 resurrectional troparion ("When Thou didst descend to death…")
had a missing closing quotation mark for as long as we've been printing it. Fixed
— the words are unchanged.

---

## 3. Two places we differ from the OCA order on purpose

Neither is an error; both are recorded as parish practice. Flagging them so they
don't look like mistakes tomorrow.

- **One Gospel, not two.** The order appoints Matthew 18:23-35 *and* Luke
  9:51-56; 10:22-24. We print Matthew only. (Both **Epistles** do print — 1
  Corinthians 9:2-12 and Colossians 1:12-18 — since doubling the epistle is the
  normal practice.)
- **The koinonikon reads "we *shall* walk"** where the OCA text has "will."
  Your correction, 8 August.

every Sunday regardless.

> or the plain Octoechos resurrectional set?**
