Liturgical Reading Cadences
How a reader ends phrases is an important technique to learn as a liturgical reader in
the Orthodox Church. If a reader is not prepared and has not looked ahead at the text,
often times, these endings, or cadences as they are also called, can leave everyone
wondering whether or not the reader has finished. Below are closing, or ending,
phrases taken from epistle and Acts readings throughout the liturgical year. Each
phrase has at least two examples of how the reader could choose to cadence the
closing phrase. The text is taken from The Apostol published by St. Tikhon's Press.
