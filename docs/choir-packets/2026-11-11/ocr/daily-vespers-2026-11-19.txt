"Lord I Call..."

All-praised martyrs
As a shining vessel of the Spirit,
you were illumined by Him, Obadiah!
With most divine vision, you were enriched by the image of prophecy:
Foreknowledge of things to come and knowledge of the truth.//
Pray that He may grant our souls peace and great mercy!

The glorious and honorable prophets
participate through communion by grace
in the very attributes and properties

--- page 5 [text] ---
DAILY VESPERS
APOSTIKHA
WEDNESDAY STICHERA
TONE 7
O glorious apostles,
pillars of the church and preachers of the truth:
ye are brightly shining lamps,
consuming every delusion with the fire of the spirit,
and enlightening all mankind with faith.
Therefore, we beseech thee,
entreat our Savior and God//
that he may grant peace to the world, and salvation to our souls.
O apostles of Christ,
and fellow workers of the Savior:
ye took up thy crosses upon thy shoulders like ploughs,
and cleansing the desolate land of the error of idolatry,
ye sowed the word of faith.//
we rightly honor you, holy apostles of Christ.
O martyrs, worthy of all praise,
sheep of Christ's spiritual flock:
ye are a living offering and acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to the Lord.
Though the earth did not cover thee, heaven received thee.
Now that ye are companions with the angels,
we entreat thee to pray with them to God our Savior,//
that He may grant peace to the world, and salvation to our souls.
