## Mapping evidence

The body is what resolves the date mapping, and this blast shows the director
using **both filename conventions in a single email**:

- `10.08.26 Daily Vespers.pdf` — **liturgical day.** "I am including *tonight*",
  sent Wed 2026-10-07, so this is the Wed-evening DV, apiDate **2026-10-07**,
  content 10-08 (Ven. Pelagia). Already held from the 2026-10-01 packet, where it
  was remapped for the same reason; the re-download was byte-identical.
- `10.14.26 Daily Vespers.pdf` — **civil evening.** "and *next Wednesday*";
  2026-10-14 *is* a Wednesday, so apiDate **2026-10-14**, content 10-15
  (Martyr Lucian of Antioch). Read as a liturgical day it would have fallen on
  Tuesday evening 10-13, contradicting both the body and the standing Wed DV.
- `10.10.26 Great Vespers.pdf` / `10.11.26 Divine Liturgy.pdf` — "Saturday",
  "Sunday"; civil evening and the day itself. No ambiguity.

Corroborated by the rector's "This Week at St. John's — October 4"
(`stjohnoftyler@gmail.com`): Wednesday DV 6:00 PM, Saturday Great Vespers 6:00 PM,
Sunday Liturgy 10:00 AM. That email also lists an **Akathist to the Patriarch
Abraham, Sat 10-10 9:00 AM** — a service we hold no music for and do not render;
not a gap in this packet.

**Note for the review:** content for 10-10/10-11 is the **Sunday of the Holy
Fathers of the Seventh Ecumenical Council**, which carries its own propers
