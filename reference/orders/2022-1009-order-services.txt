17th Sunday after Pentecost (3rd of Luke)

NOTE: Due to the coincidence of the Feast of St. Tikhon falling on this Sunday, in keeping with OCA practice, the Commemoration of the Holy Fathers of the 7th Ecumenical Council is transferred to next Sunday, October 16.

NOTE: The rubrics presented below are the commonly accepted order. However, practices may vary with local practices or dioceses. The ruling bishop should be consulted for any specific questions regarding the accepted order of services.
