Stikhera for the Preparation of Nativity
Tone 3
M
Rejoice, Isaiah, and receive the Word of God!
Prophesy to Mary the Maiden!
She is the burning bush unconsumed by the fire of the Godhead.
Adorn yourself, O Bethlehem!
Open your gates, O Eden!
Enter, O magi, and see salvation swaddled in a crib!
Behold the star shining above the cavern://
it announces the life-giving Lord Who saves the human race!
Tell us, O Joseph, how you led the childless Virgin
into the cave at Bethlehem?
after searching the prophets and hearing the angel, he said,
I am certain that Mary will wondrously give birth to God,
whom the magi from the east will worship,
offering to Him precious gifts.//
O Lord, incarnate for our sake, Glory to Thee!
Let the ends of the earth rejoice!
Behold, the Mother of God hastens to give birth to the King of all!
O Inexpressible wonder:
The infinite One begins to be in time!
The bodiless One will clothe Himself with flesh!
The cave receives Him Who holds all creation in His hand!
Rejoice, O Bethlehem!//
Creation, dance and celebrate the prefeast of our salvation!

Tone (M) M'm
Bethlehem, receive the Mother of God,
for the unapproachable Light comes to be born in you!
Angels, marvel in heaven!
Mortals, give glory on earth!
Magi from Persia, bring your three most glorious gifts!
Shepherds abiding in the fields, sing the thrice-holy hymn!//
Let all that has breath sing praise to the Maker of all!
Tone 2
Behold, the time of your salvation draws near!
Prepare yourself, O cave,
for the Virgin is coming to give her Child to the world.
Rejoice and exult, O Bethlehem of Judah,
For the Lord shall shine forth from you!
Listen, mountains and hills
and all you regions about Judah:
Christ is come to save the human race which He created//
for He is the Lover of mankind!
Tone 6
O Cave of Bethlehem,
prepare yourself to receive the Mother who bears Christ in her womb!
Manger, receive the Word who destroys the sins of mankind!
O Shepherds, keep watch!
You will bear witness to the awesome wonder!
Magi, come from Persia and bring your gifts to the King.
For the Lord has appeared from a Virgin Mother.
She bowed to Him as a servant and spoke to Him in her heart saying:
How were You conceived in me?
How did You grow in me,//
O My Savior and my God!
