Translation of the Relics of the Protomartyr and Archdeacon Stephen
"Lord I Call..."

Tone 8
O Stephen, chosen by the Apostles,
the first among the martyrs to suffer;
and the foundation of those who serve as deacons.
thou didst enlighten with unfading light
those who celebrate thy radiant feast with splendor,
giving grace and mercy to thy servants. //
By thy prayers, O martyr, guide them to everlasting life!
O Stephen, thou martyr of Christ,
thou didst receive a crown of grace on thy head,
in accordance with thy name.
Thou didst adorn thy neck with a chain of virtues as with gold,
and becamest an all-wise dwelling of the Wisdom cherished by thee,
keeping it as thy beloved.//
Therefore thou hast been honored with grace and glory.
O most honored Stephen,
first of the martyrs to suffer,
thou art crowned with grace and glory, O Apostle.
When thou wast stoned, thou didst offer a crown to our King and God,
spiritual and more valued than gold and precious stones.
Thy deeds and name are beautiful.//
Keep us all in remembrance.

APOSTICHA
Descending from heaven, to ascend the cross,
the eternal life has come for death,
to raise those who are fallen;
to enlighten those in darkness. //
O Jesus, our savior and illuminator: glory to Thee.
RESURRECTION STICHERA
TONE 8
We glorify Christ, who rose from the dead.
He accepted the human body and soul,
and freed both from suffering.
His most pure soul descended to Hades, which he spoiled.
His holy body did not see corruption in the tomb.//
He is the redeemer of our souls.
In psalms and songs, we glorify thy resurrection from the dead, O
Christ.
By it, thou hast freed us from the tortures of Hades,//
and, as God, hast granted eternal life and great mercy.
O Master of all,
incomprehensible creator of heaven and earth:
by suffering on the cross, thou hast gained pass-ion-less-ness for us.
Accepting burial, and arising in glory,
thou resurrected Adam by thy almighty hand.
Glory to thine arising on the third day.
By it, thou hast granted us eternal life, and cleansing of sins,//
as the only compassionate one.
