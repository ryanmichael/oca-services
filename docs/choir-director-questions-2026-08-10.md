## A. Three saints we rank higher than the OCA calendar does

at Vespers, the Magnification at Matins, the saint's own propers at Liturgy. The
OCA calendar puts all three a step lower, at six stichera, which is a shorter,
plainer commemoration.

Someone made these calls deliberately at some point, so I don't want to reverse
them on the strength of the calendar alone.

## C. St Herman, and a question about the Litya generally

### 6. 9 August — St Herman of Alaska

The OCA calendar ranks this day for an **All-Night Vigil**; we currently rank it
one step below, so that Great Vespers prints **without a Litya or Blessing of the
Loaves**. I set it that way because I understood we don't serve a Vigil.

I'd rather fix the underlying confusion than keep deciding this date by date.
Right now the *saint's rank* and *whether we serve a Litya* are the same setting
in the system, so I can't record that St Herman ranks for a Vigil without also
printing a Litya we don't serve. Separating them would let the calendar be
accurate while the service stays as we sing it — and would settle about eighteen
other dates at the same time.
