Leavetaking of the Dormition · Sunday of the 12th week · **Tone 3**

**Please read the first item before tonight.** Our sheet prints the wrong hymns
at Lord I Call — not missing, *wrong*, which is harder to catch mid-service. The

## 1. Lord I Call — sing the Dormition's stichera, not the ones we printed

Our sheet gives **three** Tone 4 stichera beginning *"In a divine manner thou dost
preserve and shelter from all attacks of the enemy…"*

Those are **generic hymns to the Theotokos**, not the Dormition's. Our system
found no hymns filed under 23 August and fell back to a general set. On a
Leavetaking it should be reaching back to the feast itself.

**What the order appoints:** 4 stichera of the Resurrection, Tone 3, then
**6 stichera of the Feast, Tone 1** — the Dormition's own, the ones you sang
last Saturday. They begin:

> By the divine command the God-bearing Apostles were caught up by clouds…
> Oh, the marvelous wonder! The source of Life is laid in a grave…
> Oh, the wonder of thy mysteries, pure Lady: thou wast made the throne…
> The Dominions and Thrones, the Rulers, Principalities and Powers…

Four hymns, two of them doubled to make six — as you did on the 15th.

**Two knock-on effects on the same sheet:**

- Our balance is **7 Resurrection + 3 generic**. It should be **4 + 6**. The
  Resurrection is over-represented by three and the feast under-represented by
  three.
- **The first Resurrection sticheron prints twice in a row** (*"By Thy Cross, O
  Christ our Savior, death's dominion has been shattered…"*). Sing it once.
- **Glory…** should be the Feast, Tone 1 (or the setting in all eight tones).
  We print a generic Tone 6. **Now and ever…** is correct — the Dogmatikon,
  Tone 3.

## 2. Where the feast should take Glory *and* Now-and-ever

On a Leavetaking the feast claims both halves, on one hymn. We split them in two
places and slot in a generic hymn at "Now and ever":

**Aposticha.** The order reads *"Glory… now and ever… Feast, Tone 4"* — one hymn
of the feast for both. We print the feast's hymn at Glory, then a generic
Octoechos Theotokion at Now and ever. Use the feast's for both, from your
Dormition book.

**Closing troparia.** The order reads *"Resurrectional Troparion, Tone 3 /
Glory… now and ever… Troparion of the Feast, Tone 1."* We print the feast
troparion at Glory and then add a **Resurrectional Dismissal Theotokion**
("When Gabriel announced to thee, O Virgin…"). **Simply omit that last hymn** —
the feast troparion covers both.

## 3. What is correct — don't second-guess it

The Resurrection stichera themselves, the Dogmatikon at Now and ever, the
Saturday evening prokeimenon (*"The Lord is king,"* Tone 6), the four
resurrectional aposticha in Tone 3, and the Resurrection troparion in Tone 3 are
all right.

## Why this happened, and what's being done

The Leavetaking case is a real gap in how we handle a feast's closing day: the
day inherits nothing from the feast, so a generic set fills the hole. It affects
roughly twenty dates a year, including next Sunday, where the Forerunner's
troparion and kontakion are missing for the same reason.

I found it this afternoon and deliberately did **not** patch it before tonight —
it reaches too many dates to change hours before a service. It is the top item on
the fix list and I'd rather hand you an accurate note now than a rushed sheet.

Every automated check we run passed this service clean, which is its own problem
and is being addressed alongside the fix.

**If you spot anything else off tonight, tell me — that is how the last three
real bugs were found.**
